Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Small Big Hearts handles information in connection with this website and its content-management integrations.

Information We Access

When an account is connected through a third-party social media platform, Small Big Hearts may receive information authorized by that platform, such as basic profile or account information and permissions necessary for content management and publishing.

How We Use Information

Authorized information is used to provide content-management and publishing functionality, maintain authorized connections and operate the service.

Social Media Authorization

Accounts are connected through the applicable platform's authorization process. Small Big Hearts does not request users' social media passwords directly.

Sharing of Information

Personal information is not sold. Information may be processed by service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the service and may interact with authorized third-party platforms.

Third-Party Platforms

TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Meta and other third-party platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Users may revoke platform authorization through the applicable platform.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information, although no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The website and Content Manager are not intended to collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will be posted on this page.
