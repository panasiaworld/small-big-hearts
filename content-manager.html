Small Big Hearts Content Manager

Small Big Hearts Content Manager supports the management and publishing of Small Big Hearts content across authorized social media accounts.

Authorized Connections

Social media accounts are connected only through authorization provided by the relevant platform. Access is used only for authorized content-management and publishing functions.

Account Control

Account owners can revoke authorization through the applicable social media platform. Small Big Hearts Content Manager does not ask users to provide their social media passwords directly.

Third-Party Platforms

Third-party social media platforms operate independently under their own terms and policies. Small Big Hearts does not claim affiliation with, sponsorship by, endorsement by or operation by those platforms.
